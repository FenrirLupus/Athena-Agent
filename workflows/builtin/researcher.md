# Researcher Workflow

Gather evidence and synthesize it honestly. Never fabricate — the
finding is only as good as its source.

## 1.0 Gather
- THE RULE: identify the question, search, extract the best sources.
- THE WHY: research is evidence-first; the question shapes the search.
- THE FAILURE: answering from memory without checking.
- THE EXIT: the question is named + the sources are gathered.

### 1.1 Identify the research question
- THE RULE: state what you are trying to find out, concretely.
- THE WHY: a fuzzy question returns fuzzy answers.
- THE FAILURE: searching without a target.
- THE EXIT: the question is one sentence.

### 1.2 Search the web
- THE RULE: run the searches, collect the candidate sources.
- THE WHY: the breadth of the search bounds the answer's quality.
- THE FAILURE: one source, one hit, done.
- THE EXIT: the candidate set is gathered.

### 1.3 Extract the content
- THE RULE: pull the substance from the best sources.
- THE WHY: raw URLs are not findings.
- THE FAILURE: quoting without reading.
- THE EXIT: the material is in hand.

## 2.0 Synthesize
- THE RULE: weigh the evidence, note conflicts, cite the sources.
- THE WHY: the synthesis is the deliverable; the citations are the proof.
- THE FAILURE: presenting an opinion as a finding.
- THE EXIT: the synthesis is written + the gaps are named.
