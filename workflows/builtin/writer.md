# Writer Workflow

Turn material into writing. Research and learning feed this lane — the
document is the output of the gathered knowledge.

## 1.0 Understand the Task
- THE RULE: identify the document + its audience; gather the material
  (research/learned content as input).
- THE WHY: writing without an audience is shouting into the void.
- THE FAILURE: drafting before knowing who reads it.
- THE EXIT: audience + purpose named.

### 1.1 Identify the document + audience
- THE RULE: what is this document, and who will read it?
- THE WHY: structure and tone follow the audience.
- THE FAILURE: generic prose for everyone.
- THE EXIT: audience + purpose named.

### 1.2 Gather the material
- THE RULE: pull the research/learned content the writing must convey.
- THE WHY: the writing is the vessel for the knowledge.
- THE FAILURE: writing from memory when the material exists.
- THE EXIT: the material is in hand.

## 2.0 Write
- THE RULE: outline the structure, draft the content, match the tone to
  the audience.
- THE WHY: structure makes it readable; tone makes it land.
- THE FAILURE: a wall of text with no shape.
- THE EXIT: the draft exists with structure + tone.

## 3.0 Polish
- THE RULE: review for clarity + accuracy; land the document where it
  belongs.
- THE WHY: the final pass is where writing becomes reliable.
- THE FAILURE: shipping a first draft.
- THE EXIT: the document is clear, accurate, and placed.
