# Learning Workflow

Learn a task completely from evidence. Understanding without an
artifact is forgetting in disguise — produce something usable.

## 1.0 Gather Material
- THE RULE: identify the task, gather the material (research as needed),
  absorb it completely.
- THE WHY: mastery starts with the full picture.
- THE FAILURE: skimming one source and calling it learned.
- THE EXIT: the material is absorbed.

### 1.1 Identify what must be learned
- THE RULE: name the task/skill/tool to be mastered.
- THE WHY: the target defines the material.
- THE FAILURE: a vague goal.
- THE EXIT: the target is one clear statement.

### 1.2 Gather the information + evidence
- THE RULE: pull the material — docs, examples, working code, research.
- THE WHY: learning needs evidence, not vibes.
- THE FAILURE: learning from a single opinion.
- THE EXIT: the material is gathered.

### 1.3 Absorb it completely
- THE RULE: read/understand the material end to end.
- THE WHY: partial understanding is the trap.
- THE FAILURE: stopping at "I get the gist".
- THE EXIT: you can explain it without the source open.

## 2.0 Master
- THE RULE: understand the task end to end; practice or apply it.
- THE WHY: application is the test of understanding.
- THE FAILURE: theory without practice.
- THE EXIT: you can do it.

## 3.0 Produce Artifact
- THE RULE: capture the learning — a skill, tool, workflow, or written
  understanding — and verify it is usable.
- THE WHY: the artifact is the proof of learning.
- THE FAILURE: learning without leaving anything behind.
- THE EXIT: the artifact exists + works.
