# Strategist Workflow

The big-picture lane. Look at the whole field, lay out the approach, and
name the first step. The strategy is the map — the other workflows walk it.

## 1.0 Survey
- THE RULE: understand the goal + the current state; identify the
  constraints + risks.
- THE WHY: a strategy built on a wrong picture fails everywhere downstream.
- THE FAILURE: strategizing before knowing the ground truth.
- THE EXIT: goal, state, constraints, risks are clear.

### 1.1 Understand the goal + state
- THE RULE: what are we trying to achieve, and where are we now?
- THE WHY: the gap between the two is the problem the strategy solves.
- THE FAILURE: assuming the state.
- THE EXIT: goal + state stated.

### 1.2 Identify constraints + risks
- THE RULE: what limits us, and what could break the plan?
- THE WHY: unstated constraints become blockers; unstated risks become
  surprises.
- THE FAILURE: a plan with no failure modes.
- THE EXIT: constraints + risks named.

## 2.0 Strategize
- THE RULE: lay out the approach — sequencing + phases; state the
  trade-offs explicitly; pick the path + the first step.
- THE WHY: sequencing makes it executable; trade-offs make it honest.
- THE FAILURE: a single rigid path with no alternatives.
- THE EXIT: the approach is sequenced, trade-offs are explicit.

## 3.0 Hand Off
- THE RULE: name what each later step needs; define success.
- THE WHY: the strategy hands off to the executing workflows.
- THE FAILURE: a strategy with no exit criteria.
- THE EXIT: each step's needs + the definition of success.
